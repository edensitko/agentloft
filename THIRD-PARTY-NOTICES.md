## Microsoft Rocketbox avatars

The people in the office (`public/avatars/`) are converted from the
[Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library](https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox).
The meshes, textures and animation clips were repacked into a compact binary format by
`tools/convert-rocketbox.mjs`; `docs/rocketbox-files.txt` lists the source files that were used.
